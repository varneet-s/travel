January 2023 was the first time I packed a rucksack without knowing anyone in the group. I remember standing at the junction in Gokarna, listening to the rhythmic rush of the Arabian Sea breaking against red laterite rocks.

Every morning had an unhurried cadence. We walked the coastal cliff trails connecting Kudle Beach to Om Beach, past small wooden shacks smelling of roasted coffee and wet sand. In the evenings, we chartered an open wooden boat into the estuary. As the sun melted into the ocean horizon, casting long orange ribbons across the gentle swells, the hesitation that had followed me from the city simply evaporated.

This was the journey that proved you don't need elaborate itineraries to travel well—just good companions, steady footsteps, and the willingness to let the road dictate your hours.
