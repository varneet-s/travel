June 2024 marked an important chapter in my travel journey: stepping forward as a Trip Leader for [Raw Diaries](https://www.instagram.com/rawdiaries.in/). Leading a group is an entirely different art form than traveling solo. Your eyes are no longer just looking at the view; they are checking on everyone's energy, water levels, footing on steep scree, and comfort in the group.

We took the group beyond the usual crowded spots to high ridgelines overlooking Bahu village and hidden river confluences. Standing on a rocky promontory overlooking the vast green folds of the Seraj valley, watching twenty travelers connect and lose their city reservations, was deeply fulfilling.

From managing transport breakdowns on narrow mountain passes to facilitating late-night storytelling around the campfire, trip leadership grounded me. It showed me that the greatest hospitality is creating a space where people feel safe enough to be authentic.
