Bir in early January has a crisp, sharp clarity that disappears once the summer paragliding rush sets in. Mornings begin with frost on the pine needles, cold mist drifting along the tea gardens of Chowgan, and the low hum of chanting from the Chokling Monastery.

We spent afternoons walking the upper rim toward Billing, watching gliders trace quiet arcs against the Dhauladhar range. At sunset, the entire Kangra valley turns amber, then deep slate blue. 

Life here settles into a rhythmic simplicity: hot ginger tea, unpaved trails, and long quiet evenings by the bukhari.
