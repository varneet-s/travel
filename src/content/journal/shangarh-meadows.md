The road up from Aut into Sainj Valley narrows as it winds higher, hugging sheer rock faces before gently opening out into the natural meadow of Shangarh. Sacred to Shangchul Mahadev, the meadow is ringed by towering deodars and timber-framed Kathkuni houses.

We stayed in an old wooden homestay near the edge of the forest. The days passed without an agenda: walking forest trails carpeted in needles, drinking fresh mountain spring water, and watching afternoon clouds roll over the ridge from the Great Himalayan National Park.

Shangarh reminds you that the best places are rarely the ones built for tourism — they are the ones where daily life continues quietly around you.
