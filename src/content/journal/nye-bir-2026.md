Standing at the edge of the Bir landing ground as December 2025 gave way to January 2026 felt like closing a full circle. Exactly three years had passed since January 2023, when I first stepped onto the night bus to Gokarna without knowing where this path would lead.

Fourteen distinct journeys later—across the coastal cliffs of the south, the deep snow of Lahaul, the river ghats of Banaras, the desert dunes of Rajasthan, and the high cedar forests of Seraj—the restlessness that started it all had settled into steady gratitude.

The evening light on the Dhauladhar foothills was crisp and golden. Watching the last gliders spiral down against the orange sky, laughing with close friends wrapped in heavy woolens, I realized the most valuable thing travel teaches isn't geography—it's how to be comfortable in your own skin wherever you set down your bag.
