Three books from this month that left lasting impressions:

1. **The Snow Leopard** by Peter Matthiessen — A quiet, spiritual journey through the Dolpo region of the Himalayas. More about grief and stillness than searching for a cat.
2. **Desert Solitaire** by Edward Abbey — Reflections on seasons spent as a park ranger in southeastern Utah. Sharp, vivid observations of stone, sky, and solitude.
3. **The Old Ways** by Robert Macfarlane — Walking ancient footpaths across Britain and the Middle East, meditating on landscape, geology, and human memory.
